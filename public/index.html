How to play

Each turn, both players secretly pick an action. Attack beats Grab, Grab beats Counter, Counter beats Attack. A win deals 10 damage. Attack vs Attack is a clash (5 each); Counter vs Counter or Grab vs Grab whiffs.

Basic actions and taking hits build Smash Meter (max 8). Spend it on your fighter's skill — it wins and loses like its type, beats a basic action of the same type, and adds its bonus on a win — or save all 8 for a Final Smash (14 damage, can't be stopped).

Your opponent can see your meter and your skill's type, so mix it up.
